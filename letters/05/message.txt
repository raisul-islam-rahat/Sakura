27 September 2011
Your 5th birthday

Dear Babu Shona tah,

Happy 5th Birthday, my little baby girl. 🌸
One whole hand, five fingers high,
Five little wishes beneath your sky. ✋✨

Warm little hugs, good friends to find,
Big happy laughs and a curious mind.
Brave little questions, sweet dreams at night,
Keep asking “why?” with your eyes shining bright. 💫

And if one day you think your thoughts sound silly too,
Somewhere in your future, someone will love listening to you. ❤️
