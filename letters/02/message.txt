27 September 2008
Your 2nd birthday

Amar Baccha tah,

Happy 2nd Birthday, my little star. ✨
Tiny little shoes, messy little hair,
Running after toys without a single care. 🧸

Wobbly little steps, fingers holding tight,
Curious little eyes, sparkling so bright.

I wasn't there to watch you play,
Or hear your tiny laugh at that day.
But if I could turn back time somehow,
I'd scoop little you in my arms right now. ❤️

One day, I'd cross the stars for you
But today, my Jaan, just take your steps... one and two. 🌸
