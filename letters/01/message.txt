Your 1st birthday

Dear {{nickname}},

Hello, tiny birthday girl. Today you turn one, and somewhere there is a cake you are probably more interested in touching than eating. I did not know you then, so this is a wish sent gently backwards: may you be held close, kept warm, and given every reason to giggle. The world has had you for one whole year. What a lovely head start it had on me.
