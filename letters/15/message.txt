Your 15th birthday

Amar Adorer pakhi, 🌷

Fifteen today, with a thousand dreams to chase,
No need to hurry—life will find its pace.

If I could send one little parcel through the years,
I'd fill it with your favourite treats and happy little cheers. 🎁

And somewhere inside, a tiny note from me
“Keep dreaming, keep smiling, be whoever you want to be.”

Make a wish, taste the frosting, smile the whole day through,
I wasn't there back then… but somehow, I'm still cheering for you. ❤️
