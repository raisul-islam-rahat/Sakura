Your 10th birthday

Amar Chotto Rajkonna, 👑

Ten candles glow,
Ten years to show. ✨
A brand-new digit,
Look at you grow.

But don't grow up
Too fast, too soon.
There's time for wishes,
Stars and the moon. 🌙

Play silly games,
Dream impossible things.
Believe in magic,
And what tomorrow brings.

The stars can wait.
Your dreams can too.

And somewhere in your future,
I'll be waiting for you. ❤️
