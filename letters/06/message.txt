Your 6th birthday

Amar Chotto Pori,

Happy 6th Birthday 🌸

Chotto bag kadhe niye, school-e jawar pala,
Notun khata, rongin pencil, shopno bhora mala. 🎒✨

Tiffin box e moja thakuk, class e bondhu onek,
Chhutir ghonta bajlei jeno khushi hoy monta onek. 💕

Porte porte khelo abar, dustumi-o koro,
Hashi-khushi chotto Pori, aste aste boro hoyo. 🌸

If I could send one little note to your schoolbag that day,
It would say, “Amar Chotto Pori, smile—I’ll find you someday.
