Your 14th birthday

Amar Adorer Pagli, 🌸

Fourteen today, and so much fun ahead,
New songs to love, new dreams in your head. ✨

Try something crazy, laugh when it goes wrong,
Dance a little louder to your favourite song. 💕

Collect happy moments, adventures big and small
And may this year give you a hundred reasons 
to smile through it all. ❤️
