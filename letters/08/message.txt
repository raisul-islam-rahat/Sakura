27 September 2014
Your 8th birthday

Amar Mishti Putul, 🌸

Happy 8th Birthday! 🎂
Eight little candles, eight wonderful years,
Of colours and drawings, giggles and cheers. 🎨✨

Draw funny little animals nobody can name,
Then proudly show everyone your masterpiece all the same.
Ask a hundred questions, create whatever you please,
And celebrate your little wins with the biggest “Yippee!” 💕

Make things just because they make your heart glow,
And never lose that little joy as you grow.

Keep some of that sparkle as the years pass by
I still see it today whenever happiness lights up your eyes. ❤️
