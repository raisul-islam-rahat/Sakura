27 September 2023
Your 17th birthday

Amar Shopno Konna, 🌸

Seventeen today, with tomorrow drawing near,
So many dreams ahead, so much waiting there. ✨

Don't race the future; let it come your way,
Keep a little courage for every brand-new day.

And somewhere ahead, two separate roads will meet
Two strangers, one moment, one story yet to be. ❤️

You don't know me yet, and I haven't found you too,
But how lucky I'll be when my road finally leads to you.
