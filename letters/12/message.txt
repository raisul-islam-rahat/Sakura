27 September 2018
Your 12th birthday

Amar Chotto Mon Pakhi, 🌸

Chotto aynay dekho aaj mishti ekta mukh,
Oi mishti hashi tei jeno lukiye thake shukh. 🪞💕

Hashi te bhore uthuk tomar protita shokal,
Shopno gulo dana meluk, chhuye jak akash. 🌸

Bondhu, moja, dustumi te rongin hok din,
Chotto chotto khushi gulo thakuk chirodin. ✨

Nijeke kokhono chotto kore dekho na tumi,
Karon tumi jemon acho temon i bhishon dami. ❤️

And if little you ever wondered whether she was special…
I wish I could whisper, 
“Just wait till I find you.” ❤️
