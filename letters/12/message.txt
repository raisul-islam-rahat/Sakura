27 September 2018
Your 12th birthday

Amar Chotto Mon Pakhi, 🌸

Twelve already? Look at you growing up,
Still my tiny bundle of giggles, dreams, and mischief. 💕

May your days bring easy friends, silly laughs, and happy little surprises,
And may you always see yourself with kinder eyes than any mirror does.

Because a mirror can only show your face,
It can’t show your soft heart, 
your funny thoughts, 
or the magic you carry inside. ✨

And if little you ever wondered whether she was special…
I wish I could whisper, 
“Just wait till I find you.” ❤️
