27 September 2013
Your 7th birthday

Amar Mishti Pakhi,

Happy 7th Birthday 🌸
Seven little candles glowing your way,
For seven little years of laughter and play. 🎂

May you find a best friend who waits just for you,
And a favourite little game you could play the whole day through.
Run till you're tired, laugh till it's late,
Then race back home before dinner can wait. 🌅

And whenever you stumble or something goes wrong,
Try one more time—you’re learning all along.
No need to know everything, no need to get it right,
Just keep being curious, my little light. ❤️
