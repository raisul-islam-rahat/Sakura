27 September 2009
Your 3rd birthday

Amar Jaan Pakhi,

Happy 3rd Birthday, my little sunshine. ☀️
Three tiny candles, three years of you,
A pocket full of treasures, and adventures too. 🌸

A pretty little stone, a leaf you had to keep,
A hidden piece of candy you promised not to eat. 🍬
A hundred little questions, a thousand reasons “why?”,
And bedtime stories longer, before you close your eyes. 🌙

Run, play, make a mess, laugh the whole day through,
And may every little moment bring more smiles to you. ❤️

Just be that little Muno, curious and free,
With that tiny smile as sweet as it could be. ❤️
