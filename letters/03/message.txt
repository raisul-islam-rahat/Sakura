Happy 3rd Birthday

Tin bochorer chotto tumi, mishti ekta hashi,
Pocket bhora chotto treasure, dustumi rashi rashi. 🌸

Shundor ekta pathor, ekta pata khub priyo,
Lukiye rakha candy-ta—“Eta Tmr, na dio!” 🍬

100 chotto proshno, shob kichute “Keno?”
Golpo shunte shunte bolbe, “Aro ektu bolo!” 🌙

Dustu hoye ghure berao, hesho mon khule,
Chotto chotto khushi gulo joma rakho du-haat bhore. ❤️

Just be that little Muno, curious and free,
With that tiny smile as sweet as it could be. ❤️
