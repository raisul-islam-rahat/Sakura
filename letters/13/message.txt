27 September 2019
Your 13th birthday

Amar Mishti Dushtu, 🌸

Thirteen today—a teenager at last,
New little dreams, while childhood slips past. ✨

Try something new, keep what you adore,
Change your mind, then change it once more.

Be silly, be curious, just grow at your pace
Every version of you has its own little grace.

And every little version, old and new,
Was slowly becoming the girl I'd someday find in you. ❤️
