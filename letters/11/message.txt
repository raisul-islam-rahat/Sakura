27 September 2017
Your 11th birthday

ogo amr chotto Muno Ta, 🌸

Eleven looks beautiful on you, with a bigger world waiting ahead.
May you find friends who love the real you, in every mood and shade.

When you have a story to tell, I hope someone always stays to hear,
Someone who makes your smallest thoughts feel important and dear.

I wasn't that someone then.
I wish I could have been.

Maybe one day,
I'll make up for the years in between. ❤️
