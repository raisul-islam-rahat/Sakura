# Project artwork preferences

- User preference (2026-09-12): Never generate green-screen or other chroma-key backgrounds for this project. New character and gift art must have genuine alpha transparency and ship as lossless transparent WebP. Preserve generated alpha; do not substitute painted checkerboards or remove a green matte.
- Prioritize detailed faces and clean silhouettes at the actual in-game zoom. Do not claim upscaling adds native detail.
- Hand-kiss poses must place the boy's lips against the surface of the girl's hand, without overlapping or penetrating it. Animation frames must be consecutive with stable scale, floor alignment, anatomy, and costume.
